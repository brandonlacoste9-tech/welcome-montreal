What we collect

Welcome Montreal (montrealguide.ca) is a free city guide. We do not require accounts and we do not collect names, email addresses, or other personal information from visitors. If you contact us by email at info@montrealguide.ca, we receive only what you choose to send.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand aggregate readership (pages viewed, approximate region). This data is never sold and never tied to your identity.

External links

Our guide links to restaurants, venues, hotels, and transit providers. Their privacy practices are their own; we encourage you to review them.

Children

This site is a general-audience city guide and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
