Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

What we collect

Welcome Montreal (montrealguide.ca) is a free city guide. We do not require accounts and we do not collect names, email addresses, or other personal information from visitors. If you contact us by email at info@montrealguide.ca, we receive only what you choose to send.

Storage, advertising and external services

Advertising is currently disabled: these pages do not load the AdSense advertising script. We retain a non-executable publisher verification tag. This policy will be updated before advertising is re-enabled.

The city guide stores your language preference in your browser’s local storage. It can be cleared through your browser settings. The site loads fonts, map resources, weather information and some images from external providers, which receive connection information such as your IP address when your browser requests them. Our hosting provider also processes requests to deliver the site.

There is no analytics script in the current site. We do not require an account. Information sent by email is used to respond to your enquiry.

External links

Our guide links to restaurants, venues, hotels, and transit providers. Their privacy practices are their own; we encourage you to review them.

Children

This site is a general-audience city guide and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@montrealguide.ca.
