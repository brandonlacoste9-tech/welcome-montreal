About Welcome Montreal

Welcome Montreal is an independent city guide to Montreal, Quebec — built for visitors who want the real city, not the tourist brochure.

Every section is curated by a Montrealer: the restaurants we'd actually take you to, the pubs and venues where the night really happens, the attractions worth your limited time, and a live events calendar refreshed every morning from Ticketmaster, Tourisme Québec, and the City of Montreal's public calendar.

Our partner-hotels directory lists hotels that display our guide for their guests. Listings are free during our pilot — email info@montrealguide.ca to be featured.

Welcome Montreal is free to read and supported by advertising.
